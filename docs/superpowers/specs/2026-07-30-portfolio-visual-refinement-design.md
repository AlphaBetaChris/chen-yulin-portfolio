# Portfolio Visual Refinement Design

## Goal

Refine the existing bilingual personal portfolio without changing its established editorial identity. The result should feel cleaner, read more comfortably on common laptop screens, avoid awkward Chinese line breaks, and provide a verifiable GitHub entry point.

## Approved Direction

The site keeps its warm paper background, dark green ink, restrained rust accent, serif display typography, portrait, grid-based information hierarchy, and single-page structure.

The refinement removes the decorative vertical guide lines generated by the page background and removes the full-height borders between section labels and section content. Separation will come from whitespace, horizontal rules, and the existing section backgrounds.

## Typography and Rhythm

- Apply balanced wrapping to large display sentences so Chinese phrases do not leave one or two orphaned characters on a new line.
- Reduce the largest supporting display sizes slightly at desktop widths.
- Increase small body and metadata legibility through modest font-size and color adjustments.
- Reduce section padding, award-row height, project-card height, and skills-card height by roughly 10–15%.
- Give section labels enough horizontal space to avoid cramped three-line headings while retaining the editorial sidebar composition.

## Links

- Expose it in the hero actions and contact area in both languages.
- Open the external profile in a new tab with `rel="noreferrer"`.
- Do not invent source-code or demo URLs for the two résumé projects because no verified public URLs are available.

## Responsive Behavior

- Desktop keeps the two-column editorial sections with no vertical divider.
- Tablet keeps the existing stacked project cards.
- Mobile keeps the single-column flow and horizontal section-heading separator.
- The email address remains safely wrappable on narrow screens.

## Accessibility

- Existing semantic sections, headings, language toggle, focus states, email link, and phone link remain intact.
- The GitHub link has a localized accessible label.
- Reduced-motion and print behavior remain supported.
